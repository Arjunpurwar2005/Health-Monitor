Now the real story.

Every November, North India gets a free upgrade: air you can chew. 🌫️
The AQI app has 5 colours: green, yellow, orange, red and "bhai saans mat lo."

For us it's a sore throat and an Instagram story.
For elderly parents it can mean breathlessness, falling oxygen levels and a hospital visit.

And then there's the Indian parents' official health report, delivered over the phone:

Me: "Tabiyat kaisi hai?"
Mummy: "Ekdum badhiya."
(Background: Papa coughing and asking where the Vicks is.)

So I built a health monitor for mine. 🔧

What it does:
• Measures heart rate and SpO2 (blood oxygen level)
• Measures body temperature
• Shows the readings on a small screen on the device
• Sends live readings to my phone and laptop, from anywhere in the world
• Sends a phone alert if temperature, oxygen level or heart rate goes outside the safe range
• Lets you change the WiFi from a phone, with no code changes

Built with: ESP32 · MAX30102 · DS18B20 · SSD1306 OLED · Blynk · WiFiManager

🚀 Coming next: a check-up button.
One big button. Press it, the device takes proper readings and saves them. After a few weeks there's a real health report to show the doctor.

Disclaimer: this is not a medical device and it doesn't replace a doctor.
It just replaces "theek hoon" with actual numbers.hlo
